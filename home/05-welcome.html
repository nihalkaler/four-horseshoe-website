Welcome to The Shoes

Your local on Burrow Hill Green

The Four Horseshoes – The Shoes to our regulars – sits on Burrow Hill Green at the edge of Chobham Common. It's an independent freehouse with all of its old character intact: a bar for a quick pint, dining rooms for a long lunch, and a heated Stable and terrace for when the sun's out (or isn't).

Our kitchen cooks seasonal British food from 12 noon every day, from 28-day dry-aged steaks to proper Sunday roasts. Book ahead for the dining room, or just drop in – walk-ins are always welcome in the bar.
