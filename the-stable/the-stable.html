Outdoor Dining

Our outdoor dining areas are covered and heated. The Stable offers an alternative area to dine. Whilst the area is covered & heated, we advise all guests to dress appropriately for the UK weather. Feel free to bring your own blankets & hot water bottles – and don’t forget your woolly hat! Our terrace has a porch area with awnings & heaters, plus a couple of tables under a large parasol, also with heaters.
