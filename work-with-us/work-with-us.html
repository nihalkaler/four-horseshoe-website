Our goal at The Four Horseshoes is to go above and beyond our customers’ expectations to provide a memorable experience, whether dining with us, drinking, experiencing our website or in conversation over the phone.

We want to assist our team achieve professional goals whilst working at The Four Horseshoes. Team members will be given the opportunity to broaden skills from day one of employment and we will always be supported in this learning process.

We are always looking for dedicated, passionate individuals with a personality who are striving to build their career in the hospitality industry.

If you would like to be part of The Four Horseshoes Team the rewards for the successful applicants will be working in a safe, professional, challenging environment where the standards are set very high. Establishing yourself as an integral part of the team resulting in personal and financial benefits. We offer competitive packages to the right candidates.

The Four Horseshoes is committed to equal opportunities, all employees, job applicants and customers will be given equal and impartial treatment, without regard to their race, colour, religion, nationality, ethnic origin, gender, sexual orientation, marital status or disability.
